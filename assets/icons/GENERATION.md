# Icono TR

Creado con la skill imagegen y la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes (sin CLI).
El original se conserva en `tikreader-original.png`; las variantes PNG e ICO se exportaron con System.Drawing.
El ICO contiene tamaños de 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 y 256 px.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production app icon for TikReader, favicon and Windows installation shortcut, square 1024x1024.
Primary request: Create one bold distinctive TR monogram. Exact text: "TR". Heavy geometric uppercase sans-serif lettering, immediately readable at 16 pixels. White letter faces with restrained cyan #00f5d4 and pink #ff2d55 offset edge accents, matching the application's dark streaming studio interface. Flat near-black #0a0a0f full-bleed square background. Center the monogram with all letter details inside the central 70 percent of the canvas for safe app icon masking. Crisp clean flat graphic, confident simple silhouette, optical balance, strong contrast.
Constraints: Single icon only, straight-on, no mockup, no border, no additional text, no tiny details, no shadows outside the letters, no watermark, no musical note. Opaque background extends to every edge.
